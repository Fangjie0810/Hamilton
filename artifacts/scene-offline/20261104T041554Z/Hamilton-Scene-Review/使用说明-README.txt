Hamilton · 三场景离线体验

1. 解压 ZIP，用电脑浏览器打开 Hamilton-Scenes.html。无需联网、安装开发软件或启动服务器。请勿在聊天软件的文件预览中运行。

2. 右上角切换纽约街头、酒馆和舞会。WASD 行走，按住 Shift 奔跑；也可点击地面，或通过“步行探索”选择目的地。

3. 右键拖动或使用右下角视角球调整方向。滚轮或缩放条调节 100%–140% 视距。人物经过门口进入室内后，屋顶和挡住视线的墙才会让开。

4. 每个场景有四件收藏品。勾选“收藏品”控制显示，走近后按 E 查看模型和附文；收入收藏册后可再次阅读。收藏品尚未连接主线、支线或结局。

5. “保存画面”下载当前场景 PNG；“导出模型”下载完整静态 GLB。静态模型不包含人物动画、连续行走和进出透视，灯光在其他查看器中可能有所不同。

6. 语言菜单可切换中文、英文和两种双语模式。Esc 暂停。关闭收藏品不会清空记录；更换浏览器、移动文件或使用隐私模式后，记录可能不会保留。

这是三个场景的可探索演示，适合电脑键盘和鼠标。本包不包含完整剧情、音乐和配音。所有场景和人物图片已放在 HTML 内，不会自动上传或发送文件。

Hamilton · Three scenes offline

1. Extract the ZIP and open Hamilton-Scenes.html in a desktop web browser. No network, development tools or server are needed. Do not run it inside a chat app file preview.

2. Switch between New York, Tavern and Ballroom at the top right. Move with WASD, hold Shift to run, click the ground or choose a destination under Explore on foot.

3. Right-drag or use the orbit control to rotate the camera. Use the wheel or slider for 100%–140% zoom. Roofs and foreground walls reveal the interior after you enter through a doorway.

4. Each scene has four keepsakes. Toggle Collectibles, approach an item and press E to inspect its model and note. Keep it in the Collection to revisit the note. Keepsakes are not yet connected to story branches or endings.

5. Save view downloads a PNG; Export model downloads a complete static GLB. Static models exclude character animation, walking and doorway cutaways. Lighting may look different in other viewers.

6. The language menu offers Chinese, English and two bilingual modes. Esc pauses. Hiding collectibles preserves progress; changing browsers, moving the file or using private browsing may not preserve it.

This is an explorable three-scene demo for a desktop keyboard and mouse. It does not include the full story, music or voiceover. All scenery and character images are embedded in the HTML; it does not upload or send files automatically.
